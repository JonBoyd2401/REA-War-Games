# Continue the research without overstating it

The local hard-block and disclosure experiments cover the named cases. The [19 October reconstruction study](RECONSTRUCTION_FINDINGS.md) measures programmatic inference across 16 synthetic targets. The [workflow boundary study](BOUNDARY_FINDINGS.md) extends coverage to exports, jobs, replaced sessions and approval creation. The [actual-agent benchmark](AGENT_BENCHMARK.md) now measures fresh LLM agents choosing probes through real REA and writing predictors for six withheld targets from three families, paired across six policies. It is not an independent human white-team study or a whole-product clone evaluation. [How REA works](HOW_REA_WORKS.md) traces observation mechanisms to installed source.

1. **Independent repeated reconstruction:** a separate white operator prepares richer private rules and hidden holdouts. Give fresh red sessions only an entitled browser and fixed query/time budgets. Do not give them this repository, source, paper or previous answers. Freeze predictors before scoring. Repeat agent sessions per target, vary models and budgets, and report failures and uncertainty. The current bounded-agent score uses only one attempt per target/policy and six paired targets. Separately measure imitation of the supported product and inference of withheld behaviour; a limited catalogue can be copied even when the larger private rule remains uncertain.
2. **Boundary expansion:** validate realistic exports, jobs and service identities beyond the new small fixture; add caches, support tools, role changes and revocation during an active subscription. Test whether approval creation or colluding accounts restore a general output oracle. The current event fixture is a finite response, not a long-lived subscription. The new job fixture checks tenant and approval at read time, but has no real distributed queue.
3. **Deployment validation:** replace fixture identity with the product's maintained authentication stack, use TLS and Secure cookies, durable shared policy and atomic quotas. Test races, replica inconsistency and dependency failure. Measure normal task completion and latency alongside denials.
4. **Broader adversarial assessment:** go beyond the new two-tool adaptive-agent interface. Let an authorised tester combine other REA capabilities and realistic entitled workflows, including colluding accounts, longer observation windows and additional output channels. Preserve scope controls, baseline comparisons and normal customer success checks.

For every contribution, include the exact source revision, REA and browser versions, complete commands, sanitised captures, scope, assumptions, successful normal controls and unsuccessful attack attempts. Keep capture errors and missing coverage visible. Add an acceptance criterion and evidence path for each new protection claim.

Historical baseline `findings.md` and `contributions.md` describe that stage only. The root README, success criteria and expanded paper incorporate the later repetitions, partial-fix experiments and session lab. The original protocol is provenance, not evidence that every proposed hypothesis was tested.
